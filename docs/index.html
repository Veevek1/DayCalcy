Seven calculators, one app

Each one gives a clear answer, and every result is saved to History so you can reopen it later.

  • Age CalculatorYour exact age in years, months and days, on any date.
  • Date DifferenceDays between two dates, with an option to count only business days.
  • Add / Subtract DateAdd or subtract years, months, weeks and days from a date.
  • Day CounterCount days from or to a date, with or without the end date.
  • Day of WeekSee which weekday any date falls on.
  • Duration CalculatorTime between two dates and times, with optional break deduction.
  • Date ReminderGet a notification for birthdays, deadlines and anniversaries.

Privacy

DayCalcy does not ask for internet access and has no account or sign-in.

  • Your reminders, calculation history, holidays and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The notification permission is used only to show your reminders. The alarm permission is used only so reminders arrive at the time you set. The run-at-startup permission is used only to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's saved data in your own Google backup. DayCalcy does not receive it.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.
