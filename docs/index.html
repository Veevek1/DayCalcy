DayCalcy

Calculate dates easily

Find your exact age, count the days between two dates, or set a reminder for a date that matters. Works offline.

Nine tools in one app

Every result is saved in History, so you can open it again later.

  • Age CalculatorYour exact age in years, months and days, on any date.
  • Date DifferenceDays between two dates, with an option to count only business days.
  • Add / Subtract DateAdd or subtract years, months, weeks and days from a date.
  • Day CounterCount days from or to a date, with or without the end date.
  • Day of WeekSee which weekday any date falls on.
  • Duration CalculatorTime between two dates and times. You can take off break time.
  • Value TrackerStart with a number and add or subtract an amount every day, week, month or few days. See what the value is today, and set a target if you like.
  • Date ReminderGet a notification for birthdays, deadlines and anniversaries.
  • To-Do ListWrite your tasks and tick them off. Add a reminder to any task.

How it looks

The same app in light and dark theme.

Reminders

Get a notification on the day. Snooze it or mark it done from the notification.

Calendar and history

See a monthly calendar with your holidays and reminders. Open old calculations any time.

To-do list

Add tasks and set a reminder for any of them. Press and hold a task to move it.

Light, Dark or AMOLED

Pick the theme you like. On Android 13 and up, the app icon can follow your theme too.

Home screen widgets

Keep your dates and values on the home screen, so you do not need to open the app.

  • To-do listTick off tasks or add a new one. You can make the widget bigger or smaller.
  • Year progressShows how many days of the year have passed and how many are left.
  • CountdownCounts down to a date or reminder you choose.
  • Value trackerSave a tracker and put it on your home screen. It shows today's value and how close you are to your target.

Privacy

DayCalcy does not ask for internet access and has no account or sign-in.

  • Your reminders, calculation history, holidays and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The notification permission is used only to show your reminders. The alarm permission is used only so reminders arrive at the time you set. The run-at-startup permission is used only to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's saved data in your own Google backup. DayCalcy does not receive it.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.
