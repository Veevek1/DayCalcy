DayCalcy is a simple, private and completely offline date calculator with reminders.

Calculate the number of days between dates, add or subtract days, calculate age, compare dates and work with time durations.

Save important dates as reminders and get notified when they arrive.

DayCalcy works without an internet connection and does not require online services.

DayCalcy also includes Countdown Widgets for tracking upcoming events and a Year Progress Widget to view the progress of the current year directly from your home screen.
